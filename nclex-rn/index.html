How this exam works

  • Adaptive. Starts near the passing standard, then moves to harder items after correct answers and easier items after incorrect ones.
  • Variable length. Minimum 75 items, maximum 145 items.
  • 95% confidence rule. The exam stops as soon as it is 95% certain your ability is above or below the passing standard.
  • 5-hour limit including optional breaks.
  • No going back. Each item must be answered before you can advance, exactly as on the real exam.

Item types included

  • Multiple choice
  • Select all that apply
  • Ordered response (drag)
  • Drag & drop grouping
  • Bow-tie
  • Matrix / grid
  • Cloze drop-down
  • Highlight text
  • Unfolding case studies

Case studies are delivered as six linked items that follow the NCSBN Clinical Judgment Measurement Model.
